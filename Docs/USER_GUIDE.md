# StudySpot - User Guide

**Welcome to StudySpot!**  
StudySpot helps students find productive study places, keep personal notes about Wi-Fi, power, and noise, and track completed study sessions.

---

## 1. Exploring Study Spots (Screen 01: Explore)

When you launch StudySpot, you land on the **Explore** tab.

### Searching for Places
1. **Search Bar:** Type any venue name or keyword (e.g., "coffee", "library", "reading") in the search field. The search responds responsibly with a brief debounce pause so you don't overwhelm network resources.
2. **Category Filters:** Tap the **All**, **Libraries**, or **Cafés** chips below the search bar to filter venues immediately.
3. **Changing Region:** Tap the area pill (e.g. **Colombo v**) at the top of the screen to switch between preset regions like Colombo, Kandy, Galle, or Negombo.
4. **Bookmarking a Spot:** Tap the bookmark icon on any card to save it to your personal **Saved Places** tab. Tap it again to unsave.
5. **Viewing Details:** Tap anywhere on a place card to open its full **Place Details** screen.

---

## 2. Interactive Map (Screen 02: Map)

The **Map** tab lets you visualize study spaces geographically.

1. **Viewing Map Pins:**
   - Book pins represent **Libraries**.
   - Cup pins represent **Cafés**.
   - Tapping any pin highlights it with a warm golden circle and opens a preview card at the bottom of the screen.
2. **"Search this area":** Pan or zoom the map to any neighborhood. Tap the floating **"Search this area"** pill button at the top to discover study spots currently visible in your camera frame.
3. **"Near Me" Location Button:** Tap the circular crosshair button at the bottom-right. When tapped for the first time, iOS asks for permission (*"While Using the App"*). StudySpot then smoothly centers the camera on your current location. If permission is declined, manual exploration remains fully functional!
4. **"View place":** Tap the **"View place"** button on the bottom preview card to push directly to the place details.

---

## 3. Place Details & Logging Sessions (Screen 03: Place Details)

The **Place Details** screen gathers your personal study experiences for a specific spot.

1. **Save / Saved:** Tap the **"Save place"** button to bookmark this venue, or **"Saved"** to remove the bookmark.
2. **Directions:** Tap **"Directions"** to launch Apple Maps directly with the exact walking or driving route to the venue.
3. **Your Study Notes & Observations:**
   - View chips for **Wi-Fi** (Available / None / Limited / Unknown), **Power Outlets** (Plentiful / Few / None / Unknown), and **Noise Level** (Quiet / Moderate / Lively / Unknown).
   - Tap the **pencil icon** next to "Your study notes" to update your observations or type a private note (up to 500 characters).
4. **Logging a Completed Study Session:**
   - Tap **"+ Log study session"**.
   - Select the date and time, duration (using preset chips like 30m, 45m, 60m or the stepper from 1 to 240 minutes), focus rating (1 to 5 stars), and optional notes.
   - Tap **Save**. The recent session preview and your progress totals update immediately!
5. **Permanent Deletion:**
   - Tap the three dots (`...`) in the top right navigation bar.
   - Select **"Delete Local Place Record"**. A dialog confirms the action and discloses the exact number of linked study sessions that will be removed.

---

## 4. Managing Saved Places (Screen 04: Saved Places)

The **Saved Places** tab organizes the spaces you love.

1. **Instant Search:** Use the search bar at the top to search through your saved spots by name, address, category, or personal note text.
2. **Personal Note Previews:** Each card displays your custom note (e.g., *"Your note: quiet corner by the window"*).
3. **Quick Note Editing:** Tap the small pencil icon on any card to edit your observations without leaving the screen.
4. **Swipe to Unsave:** Swipe left on any card or tap the bookmark icon to remove the spot from your saved list. **Your logged study sessions and history at that spot remain completely safe and intact in My Progress.**

---

## 5. Tracking Your Study Progress (Screen 05: My Progress)

The **My Progress** tab helps you understand your focus and study habits.

1. **Period Selector:** Tap the dropdown at the top to view metrics for **This week**, **This month**, or **All time**.
2. **Hero Study Time:** The large deep-teal card shows your accumulated study time (e.g., **3h 30m**).
3. **Stat Grid:** Displays total completed **Sessions** and **Average focus** (e.g., **4/5** stars).
4. **Weekly Study Chart:** An accessible bar chart illustrating study minutes distributed across Monday through Sunday for the current week.
5. **Editing Recent Sessions:** Tap the **pencil icon** on any recent session row to adjust the duration, date, focus rating, or notes.
6. **Deleting Sessions:** Tap the three dots (`...`) on any session row and choose **"Delete Session"** to delete it. All aggregate stats and charts refresh immediately.
